# AsterNova — Scope & Evidence Boundaries

This document records the claims intentionally **not** made by the AsterNova Salesforce Revenue Operations System until the supporting evidence exists.

## 1. Requirements Gathering

**Claim status:** Not claimed.

AsterNova is a self-directed simulation, not a stakeholder-interview engagement. The project therefore demonstrates **Requirements Translation/Definition**, not end-to-end stakeholder Requirements Gathering.

What is demonstrated:
- simulated operating needs translated into process maps
- GTM Rules of Engagement and decision rules
- Salesforce fields, validation rules, Flows, routing and permissions derived from those requirements
- requirements-to-UAT traceability

What is not demonstrated:
- live stakeholder discovery interviews
- workshop facilitation with business teams
- stakeholder sign-off or requirements approval cycles

## 2. Source Attribution

**Claim status:** Planned.

The project tracks Lead Source and includes a design for source-attribution reporting, but dedicated attribution reporting is not presented as completed implementation evidence yet.

Attribution will only be marked as implemented after the Salesforce reports are built, tested and validated against the project dataset.

See: [Source Attribution Reporting Extension](./source-attribution-reporting-design.md)

## 3. Lead-to-Cash

**Claim status:** Not claimed.

AsterNova currently demonstrates a governed **Lead-to-Closed-Won / Customer Success handoff** lifecycle.

The validated scope includes:
- inbound Lead capture
- routing and qualification
- Lead conversion
- Opportunity progression
- proposal follow-up
- Closed Won handoff
- Closed Lost governance
- CRM reporting and UAT

The project does not currently include:
- quoting / CPQ
- contract generation or approval
- invoicing
- billing
- payment collection
- revenue recognition

For that reason, the project is not described as a complete **Lead-to-Cash** implementation.

## Claiming Principle

Only capabilities that are directly supported by configured, tested or documented evidence are presented as implemented. Planned extensions remain labeled **Planned** until they are built and validated.
